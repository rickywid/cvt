AutoCAD Subdivision Assignment (2nd semester)

  • Course: CAD Drafting II (CRX342)
    •

For this assignment we create a subdivision plan with profile views that are essential in subdivision planning as they provide detailed snapshots of the land, showing both existing and proposed conditions along with utilities like storm and sanitary systems. They help understand the terrain’s highs and lows, guiding effective road and site design. These drawings ensure that infrastructure such as manholes and pipes align correctly with the ground, preventing construction issues. Additionally, accurate profile views demonstrate compliance with regulations and facilitate approval by clearly presenting the planned project.

During this project, students learned about subdivision planning and drawing proposed and existing ground profiles. Students also learned how to design and layout storm and sanitary pipes according to varying ground elevations.

Skills learned:

  • Subdivision Planning
  • Storm Design
  • Pipe Design
  • Ground Elevation Profiles
